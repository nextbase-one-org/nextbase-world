Construction BlackBoard AI

Construction photos with a digital site board on the photo.

Confirm the site name and shoot. The app suggests board fields on the iPhone and helps with the address and the time. Boards can be 3, 4, or 5 lines. Export JPEG, HEIC, PNG, ZIP, or a PDF photo report.

View on the App Store

On the iPhone

Photos and board details are processed on the iPhone and are not sent to a NextBase server automatically. They leave the app when you export, share, or save to Photos. Stamping an address uses Apple for the lookup. A multi-device plan uses a license check that does not include the photo or the board.

Plans

There is a monthly plan for one iPhone, and multi-device plans for 3, 5, 10, 15, or 20 iPhones. The price and period are the ones shown on Apple's purchase screen. Restore with the same Apple Account.

An MLIT-style export is a candidate format only. It is not official certification.
