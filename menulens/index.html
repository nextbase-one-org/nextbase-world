MenuLens Translate

Read overseas restaurant menus with the price context still attached.

MenuLens Translate is built for overseas restaurant menus. It places translated dish names on the menu image and shows visible local prices with an approximate Japanese-yen reference, so the menu can be understood without separating the food from its price.

View on the App Store

Restaurant-menu focused

The product is positioned for the moment a traveler receives a restaurant menu abroad: understand the dish, keep the original paper layout in view, and see the visible local price together with an approximate Japanese-yen reference.

Processing and communication

Menu translation is processed on the iPhone. Internet communication is used when exchange rates are retrieved. Menu photos and menu text are not published by this website.
