TOICHI DE TSUKE DE!

A playful friend-to-friend record of “put it on the tab.”

Enter a friend and an amount, hand over the iPhone, and let your friend tap “TOICHI DE TSUKE DE!”. The photo and the card stay on the iPhone. When it is settled, finish with SPAAAN.

View on the App Store

The recorded amount stays the amount you enter

TOICHI is a playful name in this app. It is not interest, a fee, or a penalty. Time does not increase the amount you recorded. There is no bank connection, money transfer, or lending service.

On the iPhone

The record and photo stay on the iPhone. They are not uploaded to a NextBase server. Location is optional, and the record still works if you decline. The App Store lists the download as free. If Apple shows a subscription price in the app, that displayed price is the price. App Store purchases are handled by Apple.
